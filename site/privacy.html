Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Our commitment: BrightSteps Recruitment is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

1. About this privacy policy

This policy explains how BrightSteps Recruitment (ABN 71 374 775 561) ("BrightSteps", "we", "us") collects, uses, discloses and safeguards your personal information. It applies to everyone who interacts with us, including job candidates, employees, contractors, clients and website visitors.

BrightSteps is an Australian-owned workforce solutions provider based in Tamworth, NSW, led by Director Edwin Ngetich. We specialise in industrial labour hire and community care.

2. What personal information we collect

2.1 Job candidates and employees

  • Identity information: full name, date of birth, gender, photograph.
  • Contact information: address, phone number, email address.
  • Professional information: resume, employment history, qualifications, certifications, references.
  • Financial information: bank account details, tax file number, superannuation details (collected once you are engaged, not through our website).
  • Background checks: police checks, Working With Children Checks, professional licences.
  • Health information: medical certificates and pre-employment health assessments, where legally required for specific roles.
  • Right to work: visa status, citizenship information, passport details.

2.2 Client organisations

  • Business contact information.
  • Organisation details and staffing requirements.
  • Billing and payment information.
  • Service preferences and feedback.

2.3 Website visitors

  • IP address and browser information.
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site.
  • Referring website information.
  • Cookie data, only if you agree (see section 8).
  • Anything you enter in our website forms (Request staff, Apply for work and Contact).

3. How we collect personal information

  • Directly from you, through application forms, registration, interviews and correspondence, including our website forms.
  • From third parties such as referees, previous employers and screening service providers.
  • From publicly available sources, including professional networking sites.
  • Through our website and online portals.
  • While providing our services.

4. Why we collect and use your information

4.1 Primary purposes

  • Recruitment services: matching candidates with suitable work.
  • Employment management: managing employment relationships, payroll and workplace obligations.
  • Client services: providing workforce solutions to our clients.
  • Compliance: meeting legal and regulatory requirements, including workplace health and safety, tax and employment law.
  • Communication: contacting you about job opportunities, appointments and service updates.
  • Assessment: evaluating suitability for positions and conducting background checks.
  • Administration: managing our business operations and keeping records.

4.2 Secondary purposes

  • Improving our services and website.
  • Marketing our services, with your consent.
  • Research and analysis to improve our recruitment processes.
  • Protecting against fraud and maintaining security.

5. Disclosure of personal information

5.1 When we share your information

  • Client organisations: potential and actual employers, for recruitment and placement.
  • Service providers: background check companies, medical assessors, payroll processors, and IT providers (including the services that run our website forms and email).
  • Referees: people you have nominated as references.
  • Professional advisers: lawyers, accountants and business consultants.
  • Government agencies: when required by law or to meet legal obligations.
  • Emergency services: where there is a health or safety concern.

5.2 Overseas disclosure

We generally do not disclose personal information overseas. Some technology providers we use to run our website forms and email may store data on servers outside Australia. Where information is disclosed or stored overseas, we take reasonable steps to make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, and we obtain your consent where the law requires it.

We will never sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data security and storage

6.1 Security measures

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference and loss; unauthorised access, modification or disclosure; and cyber threats and data breaches. Our measures include:

  • Secure, encrypted databases and storage systems, and encrypted (HTTPS) connections on our website.
  • Access controls and authentication requirements.
  • Regular security audits and updates.
  • Staff training on privacy and data protection.
  • Physical security for paper-based records.

6.2 Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes it was collected for, to meet legal obligations and to resolve disputes. Generally:

  • Employee records: 7 years after employment ends.
  • Candidate records: up to 2 years after last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Financial records: 7 years, as required by taxation law.

After that, we securely destroy or de-identify the information.

6.3 Notifiable data breaches

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify the people affected and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

7. Your privacy rights

7.1 Access and correction

  • Access: ask for access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Update: update your contact details and preferences at any time.

7.2 Additional rights

  • Withdraw consent: for marketing communications or specific uses of your information.
  • Anonymity: deal with us anonymously or using a pseudonym where practicable. This may limit our ability to provide services, for example, we cannot place a worker we cannot identify.
  • Complaints: make a complaint about how we have handled your personal information.

7.3 How to exercise your rights

Contact our Privacy Officer using the details in section 11. We will respond to access and correction requests within a reasonable time, usually 30 days.

8. Cookies and website analytics

Our website uses essential browser storage to remember your privacy choice. With your permission, we also use analytics cookies to understand website traffic and usage patterns so we can improve the site.

Analytics cookies are only set if you select "Accept analytics" in the privacy notice. You can change your choice at any time using , or control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies does not stop the website from working.

9. Sensitive information

We only collect sensitive information, such as health information, criminal records or information about racial or ethnic origin, when:

  • you have given consent;
  • it is required or authorised by law; or
  • it is necessary for specific job requirements, for example health assessments for safety-critical roles.

Sensitive information is handled with extra care and subject to stricter security measures. Please do not include health information or criminal history in our website forms. We will ask for it securely if a role requires it.

10. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review it periodically.

12. Consent

By providing your personal information to BrightSteps and using our services, you consent to the collection, use and disclosure of your information as described in this privacy policy.
