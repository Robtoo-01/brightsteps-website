Common questions

Can you supply workers with specific tickets or licences?

Yes. Tell us what your site needs (for example a White Card, forklift licence or specific inductions) and we will match workers who hold them.

What does a general labourer cost?

Our indicative rate for a general labourer for mining or construction is $70 an hour, excluding GST. It includes training, administration and insurance costs. See pricing.

Can you supply a whole crew?

Yes. From one worker to a full crew, tell us the numbers and timing and we will confirm what we can mobilise.
