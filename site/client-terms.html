Client terms

A plain-English summary for organisations and households that book BrightSteps workers. Your written quote and our Labour Hire Services Agreement set the binding terms.

Your written quote, and any signed agreement, take priority over this summary. Questions? Call 0424 234 925.

1. Who employs the workers

BrightSteps employs the workers we supply, usually as casual employees paid under the relevant award. We pay their wages and superannuation, hold workers compensation insurance for them, and manage their screening and timesheets. You direct their day-to-day work while they are with you.

2. Quotes and bookings

Before any work starts, we send a written quote. It confirms:

  • the hourly rate for each role;
  • any minimum shift length;
  • loadings for evenings, nights, weekends and public holidays;
  • any short-notice or emergency terms; and
  • for transport, the vehicle costs.

Rates on our pricing schedule are indicative. The quote sets the rates that apply to your booking.

3. Changes and cancellations

Tell us as soon as your needs change, by calling 0424 234 925 at any time. Any cancellation notice period and charge are set out in your quote.

4. Timesheets and invoices

Shifts are recorded on digital timesheets, which you verify. We invoice weekly or fortnightly with clear line items. Payment terms are shown on your quote and invoice.

5. Safety on site

Work health and safety duties are shared between you and BrightSteps.

  • You: provide a safe workplace, a site or service induction, day-to-day supervision and any site-specific PPE, and tell us about hazards and incidents straight away.
  • BrightSteps: checks that workers hold the tickets, licences and checks the role needs, briefs them before they start, follows up any incident with you, and can withdraw a worker if a site is unsafe.

6. Screening

Every worker is screened for their role, including a national police check, and a Working With Children Check and NDIS Worker Screening Check where the role requires them. Tell us about any extra requirements, such as inductions or vaccinations, when you book.

7. Disability and community settings

BrightSteps supplies workers to providers. Your organisation remains responsible for the supports you deliver, including participants' plans and records. Supported Independent Living must be delivered by a provider registered with the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission.

8. Replacements and concerns

If a worker can't make a shift, or you are not satisfied with a worker, call us and we will work to arrange a replacement. See feedback and complaints for how we handle concerns.

9. Employing a worker directly

If you would like to employ one of our workers directly, please talk to us first. Any fee that applies is set out in your agreement.

10. Households

If you book cleaning or domestic support for your home, you have rights under the Australian Consumer Law that these terms do not exclude.
