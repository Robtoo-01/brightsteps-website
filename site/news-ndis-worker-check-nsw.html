How to get an NDIS Worker Check in NSW

Most disability support roles need an NDIS Worker Check. Here's how the NSW process works, step by step, and what to bring.

In New South Wales, NDIS worker screening is called the NDIS Worker Check (NDISWC). Most disability support roles need one. Here's how it works.

Who can apply

You can apply when you are doing, or about to start, NDIS work, because an NDIS employer has to verify your application. If you're registering with BrightSteps for disability support work, talk to us first and we'll explain what to do.

The steps

  1. Apply online through your MyServiceNSW account on the Service NSW website. You can't apply in the Service NSW phone app.
  2. Visit a Service NSW centre within 28 days of starting your application. Bring your original identity documents, pay the fee and have your photo taken.
  3. The screening agency checks your application, run by the NSW Office of the Children's Guardian.
  4. Your employer verifies your application through the NDIS Worker Screening Database.

What to bring

You need four identity documents from set categories. Check the current list in the official application guide before you go.

How long it lasts

A clearance is valid for five years. If you change employers or roles while it's current, you don't need to reapply.

Official information: Service NSW: NDIS Worker Check and Office of the Children's Guardian: how to apply.
