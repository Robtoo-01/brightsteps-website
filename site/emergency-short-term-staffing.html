Common questions

How fast can you fill an urgent shift?

We work to fill urgent shifts as quickly as possible. Timing depends on the role, location and requirements, so call 0424 234 925 and we'll tell you straight away what we can do.

Is there a minimum engagement?

We supply everything from single shifts to longer contracts. Any minimum shift length, short-notice terms and cancellation terms are set out in your quote. See our client terms.

Do urgent workers meet the same standards?

Yes. Every worker we supply is screened for their role, including a national police check, and a WWCC and NDIS Worker Screening Check where the role requires them, and covered by workers compensation and public liability insurance.
