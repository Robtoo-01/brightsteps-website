Pricing questions

What is included in your rates?

Our rates are designed to be competitive and include training, administration and insurance costs. All rates exclude GST.

Why don't all roles show a rate?

Cleaning, domestic support and support coordinator assistant rates depend on the scope of work. Request a quote and we will price it for you.

How are shifts recorded and billed?

Shift completion is verified through digital timesheets. We invoice weekly or fortnightly.

Are there extra charges for nights, weekends or public holidays?

Rates can vary with shift times, award conditions and location. Your quote will set out the rates that apply to your request before any work starts.
