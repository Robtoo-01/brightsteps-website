Common questions

What does transport and community access cost?

Our indicative rate is $65 an hour, excluding GST. The hourly rate is fixed and vehicle costs are negotiable. See pricing.

Do you provide cleaning for both homes and businesses?

Yes. We supply residential and commercial cleaners, plus domestic support with daily household tasks.

What does a support coordinator assistant do?

They provide administrative support, such as scheduling, records and timesheets, so coordinators can focus on participants.
