Where BrightSteps supplies workers

From our Tamworth office we supply labourers and support workers to towns across the New England and North West, the Hunter and Sydney.

Our office is at 6A Edinburgh Avenue, Tamworth. From here we supply screened, insured workers to sites and providers across regional New South Wales.

Towns we cover

New England & North West: Tamworth, Armidale, Gunnedah, Quirindi, Moree, Narrabri, Inverell, Glen Innes, Manilla, Werris Creek, Walcha, Uralla

Hunter: Singleton, Muswellbrook, Scone, Newcastle

Sydney: Sydney

Not on the list? Call us on 0424 234 925. We'll tell you straight away whether we can help. See the full areas we cover page.

What we supply

Industrial labour hire for mines, civil sites and warehouses, disability support workers for NDIS providers, and community services staff, including drivers and cleaners.
