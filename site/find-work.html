Job seeker FAQs

Do I need experience?

It depends on the role. Some roles need specific experience, licences or qualifications; others suit people who are reliable and keen to learn. Tell us about yourself in the application.

Which checks do I need?

Every worker needs a national police check. Roles with children need a Working With Children Check (WWCC), and most disability support roles need an NDIS Worker Screening Check. We will explain what applies to the roles you're interested in.

Who will employ me?

BrightSteps employs you, usually as a casual employee paid at least the minimum rate in the award that covers your role. We explain your pay rate and pay cycle before you start any role.

Can I choose my shifts?

Tell us your availability when you register: weekdays, nights, weekends or on-call. We'll offer shifts that match.

How do I get paid?

Your hours are recorded on digital timesheets. We will explain pay rates and pay cycles before you start any role.

Does it cost anything to register?

No. Registering with BrightSteps is free.

Ready to take the next step?

Register in about five minutes. It's free.
