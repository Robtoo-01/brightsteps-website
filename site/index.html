Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you supply staff?

Timing depends on the role, the number of workers, the location and the checks your site needs. Call 0424 234 925 for urgent requests and we will tell you straight away what we can do.

Are your workers screened and insured?

Yes. Every worker is screened for their role, including a national police check, and a Working With Children Check (WWCC) and NDIS Worker Screening Check where the role requires them. We provide workers compensation and public liability insurance for every contract.

What does it cost?

Our indicative rates start from $65 an hour (ex GST) and include training, administration and insurance. See the pricing schedule or request a quote for your exact needs.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Tamworth and supply workers across the New England and North West (including Armidale, Gunnedah, Quirindi, Moree and Narrabri), the Hunter (including Singleton and Newcastle) and Sydney. We only take on interstate work where we hold the labour hire licence that state requires. See areas we cover, or tell us where you need people and we will confirm what we can do.

I'm looking for work. How do I apply?

Register through our online application. Tell us the type of work you want and your availability, and our team will be in touch about suitable roles.
