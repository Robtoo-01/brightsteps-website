Common questions

What does transport and community access cost?

Our indicative rate is $65 an hour, excluding GST, plus vehicle costs. Vehicle costs are negotiable and set out in your quote. See pricing.

Whose vehicle is used?

It depends on the booking. Before the first trip we confirm in writing which vehicle will be used and how it is insured.

Can the worker support the participant at the destination?

Yes. Our workers can support participants during appointments and activities, as well as driving them there and back.
