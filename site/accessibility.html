Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to find work and request staff through this website, including people with disability.

Our commitment

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. As a business that places disability support workers, accessibility is part of how we work, not an add-on.

What we've done

  • Used a clear, high-contrast colour palette that meets WCAG AA contrast ratios.
  • Chose Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed for readers with low vision.
  • Built every page to work with a keyboard, with visible focus indicators and a "skip to main content" link.
  • Used meaningful headings, landmarks and labels for screen reader users.
  • Labelled every form field, with clear error messages and an error summary. There is no CAPTCHA on our forms.
  • Made the site work at 200% zoom and on small screens, and respected the "reduce motion" setting.

Known limitations

Photos on this site are currently placeholders. When we add real photography we will write descriptive alternative text for each image. Some third-party content, such as embedded maps, may not be fully accessible.

Need help or found a barrier?

If you have trouble using any part of this website, or need information in another format, please tell us. We can complete a staffing request or job application with you over the phone.

Phone: 0424 234 925
Email: info@brightstepsrecruitment.com.au

If you are deaf, or have a hearing or speech impairment, you can contact us through the National Relay Service.
