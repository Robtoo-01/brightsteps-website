## Notes

- **The Unsplash photos are stock, not BrightSteps staff or clients.** Alt text describes each scene, not "our team". Never caption a stock photo as BrightSteps people.
- **`edwin-ngetich.jpg`** (About page, Director) was supplied by BrightSteps on 23 November 2026 (400 × 400 JPEG). It is also referenced in the site's structured data as the founder's image. A larger, more natural photo (at least 800 × 800, in a real setting) would look sharper.
- The About page's team photo slot uses a stock warehouse image with a scene description; replace it with a real BrightSteps team photo when available.
- **`disability-support.webp`** was published by Care Assure, an Australian home-care provider, as part of its own marketing. The licence allows our use, but consider replacing it with your own photo so it can't be mistaken for another provider's imagery.
- **Replacing a photo:** save the new image with the same file name, at 1200 × 900 WebP, or change the file name and alt text in `src/pages/`. Get written consent from anyone pictured, especially support participants.
